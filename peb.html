››› What's Included

Complete PEB systems, engineered end to end

01

Complete PEB Systems

Primary framing (tapered columns and rafters), secondary framing (Z/C purlins, girts), and cladding — factory-fabricated and bolted at site.

02

Large Clear Spans

Clear spans up to 93 m and heights up to 54 m, supporting large, column-light operating footprints.

03

International Design Codes

Engineered to MBMA, AISC, AISI, and AWS (US) standards, plus applicable Indian and local design codes.

04

Standing-Seam Roofing

FM-Global-approved standing-seam roofing system with 360° seams and continuous runs of 150 m or more.

05

Solar-Ready Rooftops

Rooftops engineered for photovoltaic installation from the design stage, not retrofitted later.

06

Building Accessories

Canopies, fascias, mezzanines, skylights, ventilators, louvers, sliding doors, insulation, and partitions.

Complex Structural Steel

Multi-storey structures and heavy industrial frames beyond standard single-storey PEB scope.

Every Facility Type

››› Why PEB

Faster, more predictable, and built to expand

  • 30–40% faster completion than conventional construction
  • Predictable, fixed-scope cost
  • Single-point responsibility across design, fabrication, and erection
  • Factory-level quality control
  • Expandable and flexible for future growth
  • ~100% recyclable structural steel

The Kairad angle: we hold a standardized structural specification across every site in your network, so racking, MHE, and automation fit a known, repeatable footprint at site 1 and site 500 alike.
